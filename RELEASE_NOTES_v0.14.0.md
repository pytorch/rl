# TorchRL v0.14.0

TorchRL 0.14 is a major feature release spanning model-based reinforcement learning, distributed training and inference, replay-buffer composition, checkpointing, on-policy optimization, vision-language-action workflows, and simulation tooling. It also tightens several contracts announced in earlier releases and requires TensorDict 0.14.1 or later in the 0.14 release line.

## Highlights

- DreamerV3 receives an end-to-end correctness and performance overhaul, including a block-RSSM core, public `DreamerV3BlockGRU` modules, a Triton backend with a faster and more memory-efficient backward pass, corrected losses and continuation semantics, reusable percentile return normalization, and a reproducible DMC Walker benchmark. See [#4072](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4072), [#4074](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4074), [#4075](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4075), [#4125](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4125), [#4126](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4126), [#4132](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4132), [#4133](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4133), [#4158](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4158), [#4160](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4160), [#4174](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4174), and [#4196](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4196) by @vmoens and @theap06, with correctness fixes in [#4119](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4119) by @gtnv and [#4118](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4118) and [#4128](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4128) by @unography.
- Distributed execution now covers configurable TorchRL service transports, shared-memory inference, process inference servers, DDP and Ray trainer execution, remote learners, and unified collector backend construction. See [#3955](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3955), [#3958](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3958), [#4022](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4022), and the distributed trainer [commit stack](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/311c1b13f) by @vmoens.
- Replay buffers gain a trajectory query language, composable sample units, rich sequence sampling over contiguous or fragmented trajectories, offline-to-online transitions, generation-stamped slots, operational statistics, and consume-after-sample support. See [#3947](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3947), [#4045](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4045), [#4051](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4051), [#4031](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4031), and [#4180](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4180) by @theap06 and @vmoens, [#4050](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4050) by @coder-jayp, [#4046](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4046) by @harryfrzz, and [#3911](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3911) by @vmoens.
- On-policy and LLM training add `GRPOTrainer`, PPO-EWMA with a decoupled proximal policy, reusable percentile value normalization, and group-wise value-estimator standardization. See [#4140](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4140) by @coder-jayp and [#4126](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4126), [#4127](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4127), [#4212](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4212), [#4213](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4213), and [#4214](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4214) by @vmoens.
- Simulation support expands with a CraftGround Minecraft wrapper, MuJoCo contact and site APIs, the `MicroDuckEnv` multitask locomotion environment and training examples, and richer `torchrl.render` notebooks and MuJoCo WASM tooling. See [#4107](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4107), [#4183](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4183), [#4203](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4203), [#4204](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4204), [#4205](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4205), [#4212](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4212), and [#4218](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4218) by @vmoens.
- The VLA stack provides TensorDict-native VLA data and policy abstractions, LeRobot and OpenVLA integration, and LIBERO training recipes. See [#3884](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3884), [#3995](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3995), and the VLA [data](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/b6048dc43) and [LIBERO](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/ed66a0f48) commit stacks by @vmoens.

## Breaking changes

- `QValueModule` and `QValueActor` now default to strict action-shape validation. A mismatch with the action spec raises `RuntimeError`; pass `strict_shape="auto"` to reshape compatible outputs or `strict_shape=False` to disable validation. Explicit legacy `None` follows the new strict behavior. [#4179](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4179) by @vmoens.
- The deprecated `MultiCollector.postprocs` alias has been removed. Use `MultiCollector.postproc`. [#4108](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4108) by @vmoens.
- TorchRL now requires `tensordict>=0.14.1,<0.15.0`; upgrade TorchRL and TensorDict together. [#4108](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4108) and [#4212](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4212) by @vmoens.

## Deprecations and future defaults

- The legacy `use_ray_service` switch is deprecated in favor of explicit `service_backend` selection and is scheduled for removal in v0.16. [ca3261f52](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/ca3261f52) by @vmoens.
- The default `Trainer` checkpoint format will move from the legacy backend to `torchrl.checkpoint` in v0.15, and the compatibility checkpoint argument is scheduled for removal in v0.16. [#3967](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3967) by @vmoens.
- DreamerV3's legacy reward-key fallback is deprecated; write logits to the configured reward-logits key before its removal in v0.16. [#4066](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4066) by @vmoens.
- Constructing a multiprocessing `AsyncEnvPool` without an explicit `exchange` now warns that the default will change from `"queue"` to `"auto"` in v0.15. Select `"queue"`, `"shm"`, or `"auto"` explicitly to pin the desired behavior. [#4200](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4200) by @vmoens.

## Features

### Model-based reinforcement learning

- `WorldModel`, `WorldModelWrapper`, and `WorldModelLoss` provide a general TensorDict-native abstraction for model-based environments, rollouts, and joint objectives. [#3783](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3783) by @theap06.
- DreamerV3 now implements corrected symexp two-hot encoding, reward/value decoding, normalized returns in both gradient modes, slow-critic regularization, categorical KL semantics, and continuation training. [#4065](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4065), [#4066](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4066), [#4067](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4067), [#4068](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4068), [#4073](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4073), [#4074](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4074), and [#4125](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4125) by @vmoens.
- `DreamerV3BlockGRU` and `DreamerV3BlockGRUCell` expose sequence-first block-GRU modules with a Python implementation and an optional Triton backend whose backward pass avoids materializing block-expanded activations. [#4158](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4158), [#4160](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4160), and [#4174](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4174) by @vmoens.
- A reproducible DreamerV3 DMC Walker implementation, benchmark, pinned JAX comparison, and one-command reproduction script make the reference training path directly runnable and measurable. [#4075](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4075) and [#4133](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4133) by @vmoens and [#4196](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4196) by @theap06.

### Distributed training, inference, and operations

- Configurable service transports and shared-memory inference support reusable communication backends across collectors, inference servers, replay services, and weight updates. [#3955](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3955) and [#3958](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3958) by @vmoens.
- `PolicyClientModule`, `ProcessInferenceServer`, structured inference-server configs, behavior-policy version tracking, and max-inflight controls form a complete remote-policy client path. [307eb5a64](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/307eb5a64), [938d0abd1](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/938d0abd1), [cd1406e38](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/cd1406e38), and [539aa07d8](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/539aa07d8) by @vmoens.
- Trainer execution backends add DDP optimization, Ray data-parallel execution, distributed checkpointing, remote off-policy learners, and `WeightSyncScheme` publication. [311c1b13f](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/311c1b13f), [6e60c472d](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/6e60c472d), [12bef231d](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/12bef231d), and [dc6dcb8a5](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/commit/dc6dcb8a5) by @vmoens.
- Collectors can be built through a unified backend interface, while `AsyncEnvPool` gains shared-memory exchange and automatic parallel environments expose worker metadata. [#4022](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4022) and [#4093](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4093) by @vmoens and [#4109](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4109) by @theap06.
- `AsyncEnvPool(exchange="auto")` selects shared-memory transport for compatible multiprocessing environments and falls back to queues for dynamic, heterogeneous, or non-tensor schemas; `resolved_exchange` reports the selected transport. [#4200](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4200) by @vmoens.
- Pull-based monitoring now includes `ReplayBuffer.stats()`, collector statistics across local, multiprocessing, and Ray collectors, plus `LoggerMonitor` and `Every`. [#4031](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4031), [#4032](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4032), and [#4033](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4033) by @theap06.

### Replay buffers and checkpointing

- The trajectory query API introduces `traj`, `Trajectory`, predicates, filters, and iterators for selecting replay data through composable trajectory expressions. [#3947](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3947) by @theap06.
- `SampleUnit`, `Transition`, and `Sequence` separate sampling semantics from storage. Sequence sampling supports exact boundary policies, burn-in, bootstrap context, dilation, per-anchor priorities, and multidimensional storage. [#4045](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4045), [#4051](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4051), and [#4085](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4085) by @theap06 and [#4050](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4050) by @coder-jayp.
- `SliceSampler(fragmented=True, step_key=...)` reconstructs fixed-length logical trajectory slices when trajectories are interleaved or non-contiguous in one-dimensional TensorDict-backed storage. [#4180](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4180) by @vmoens.
- `OfflineToOnlineReplayBuffer`, `prefill_replay_buffer`, and `OfflineToOnlineTrainer` support staged transitions from static datasets to online collection, with a runnable SOTA implementation. [#3900](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3900) and [#3904](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3904) by @theap06.
- Round-robin writers can stamp generations to reject stale priority updates, and consuming samplers can retire data after sampling. [#4046](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4046) by @harryfrzz and [#3911](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3911) by @vmoens.
- The new `torchrl.checkpoint` package handles structured state, adapters, strictness policies, RNG state, best-checkpoint selection, rotation, retention, and dependency-version diagnostics. [#3967](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3967) by @vmoens and [#4064](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4064) and [#4098](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4098) by @theap06.

### Objectives and LLM workflows

- `RNDLoss`, the RND transform, and a PPO+RND MuJoCo implementation add intrinsic-reward training with a complete runnable example. [#3889](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3889) and [#3905](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3905) by @theap06.
- `TQCLoss` adds truncated quantile critics, list-valued critic updates, and numerical-contract coverage. [#4077](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4077), [#4078](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4078), and [#4079](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4079) by @gtnv.
- `DistillationLoss` supports token-level LLM knowledge distillation, and TRL interoperability adapters translate policies, rollouts, and training data between TRL and TorchRL. [#4038](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4038) by @theap06 and [#4070](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4070) by @coder-jayp.
- `GRPOTrainer`, `GRPOOptimizationStepper`, and `WeightSyncHook` provide a modular GRPO training loop with gradient accumulation, mixed precision, clipping, and asynchronous synchronization to distributed LLM inference engines; synchronous and asynchronous SOTA pipelines exercise the complete path. [#4140](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4140) by @coder-jayp.
- `PercentileValueNorm` tracks an exponential moving average of configurable quantiles, while `ValueNorm.scale()` exposes scale-only normalization. PPO and A2C losses accept `advantage_norm` for robust, opt-in advantage scaling. [#4126](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4126) and [#4127](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4127) by @vmoens.
- PPO losses support decoupled proximal policies through `delay_actor` and capped behavior-policy ratios through `max_importance_ratio`. Trainer and Hydra integration can update the proximal policy with `SoftUpdate` or `HardUpdate`, including a runnable PPO-EWMA configuration. [#4213](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4213) and [#4214](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4214) by @vmoens.
- `GAE`, `VTrace`, `TD0Estimator`, `TD1Estimator`, and `TDLambdaEstimator` accept `group_key` to standardize advantages or rewards independently within groups such as tasks or agents. [#4212](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4212) by @vmoens.
- Reusable value transforms and configurable chunk dimensions extend value normalization and estimator execution without changing existing call patterns. [#4096](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4096) by @vmoens and [#4003](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4003) by @lin-erica.

## Performance

- Recurrent kernels gain tiled large-hidden support, 64-bit offsets, recompute-based backward passes, narrower canonicalization, specialized GRU backward scans, and hardened Triton execution. [#3818](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3818), [#3752](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3752), [#4154](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4154), and [#4175](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4175) by @vmoens.
- DreamerV3 RSSM rollout avoids unnecessary Python and tensor work, and the block-GRU scan adds optimized recurrence plus a Triton backward pass that reduces runtime and peak activation memory. [#4132](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4132), [#4159](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4159), [#4160](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4160), and [#4174](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4174) by @vmoens.
- MuJoCo-Torch batched physics steps compile as one full graph by default when `compile_step=True`, preventing silent eager fallback after graph breaks or recompilation limits. [#4202](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4202) by @vmoens.
- `MultiSyncCollector` preemption now uses a shared-memory interruptor instead of busy-waiting, with macOS support, and stops gathering as soon as all worker outputs arrive. [#3843](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3843) and [#3841](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/3841) by @vmoens.
- Replay-boundary queries are cached and vectorized, `TransformedEnv` specs lock by default, and parent-side LIBERO construction is eliminated. [#4084](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4084) by @theap06, [#4121](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4121) by @mathieuorhan, and [#4028](https://github.com/pytorch/rl/pull/4028) by @vmoens.

## Upgrade notes

- Install matching release lines together: `pip install "torchrl==0.14.0" "tensordict>=0.14.1,<0.15.0"`.
- Review code using `QValueModule` or `QValueActor` with shaped categorical specs. Choose strict validation, automatic reshaping, or explicit opt-out rather than relying on the former warning-only default.
- Replace `MultiCollector.postprocs` with `MultiCollector.postproc`, and review the v0.15 checkpoint and collector-default warnings before the next release.
- Pass an explicit `exchange` to multiprocessing `AsyncEnvPool` instances to keep queue transport or opt into automatic shared-memory selection before the default changes.
